# Project Mission: AI Execution OS

## Problem
Team leads managing small squads (3–10 people) run recurring meetings (standups, planning, retros) but struggle to track whether decisions stick, and often lose context between sessions. Their core question is constantly: "What did we agree to, who said they'd do it, and why?"

## Target Audience
Strictly focused on the Team Lead (V1).

## Core Value Proposition
Turn every meeting from a conversation into a traceable commitment. Users can simply paste raw transcripts and instantly get structured extraction (decisions, tasks, open questions), contradiction alerts, Q&A with citations, topic threads, and accountability views.

## Success Definition
A robust, production-ready system deployed to the cloud for real teams to use immediately, functioning flawlessly as a magic "Command Center" for the team lead.

## Out of Scope / Non-Goals (V1)
- Paid APIs or dependencies (relying on open-source/free-tier LLMs like Ollama/Groq/Gemini Flash).
- Audio file upload, speaker diarization (Whisper deferred to V2).
- Third-party integrations (no Slack/email hooks, no calendar sync).
- Multi-workspace or org-level views.
